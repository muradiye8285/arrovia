01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Arrovia (“the App”), developed by Muradiye Kayaduman (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information. Arrovia is a logic puzzle game with campaign levels, daily challenges, optional Game Center leaderboards, and advertising.

We do not operate a separate user-account system or a developer-controlled database of player profiles.

02

Information stored on your device

Arrovia stores gameplay information locally so it can remember your progress and preferences. This may include completed and unlocked levels, scores, stars, daily challenge history, streaks, hints, settings, onboarding status, and review-prompt status.

This local gameplay information is not uploaded to a server operated by us. It remains until you reset progress in the App or remove the App, subject to standard iOS backup and restore behavior.

03

Advertising and consent

Arrovia uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner advertisements, occasional interstitial advertisements, and optional rewarded advertisements. A rewarded advertisement is shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game benefit such as a hint.

Google and participating advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and device or usage information. What is processed depends on your region, device settings, age-related settings, consent choices, and whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is available.

Where required, Arrovia presents Google’s consent message before requesting ads. You may revisit available advertising privacy choices from the App’s Settings screen.

04

Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Arrovia accesses your Game Center alias, player identifier, leaderboard rank, and submitted puzzle score to provide friends and global leaderboards. This feature is provided and managed by Apple. We do not receive your Apple Account password or operate a separate copy of the Game Center account database.

Game Center information is handled according to Apple’s privacy practices and your Apple Account and Game Center settings. You can disable Game Center access through your device settings.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy ↗

05

Data sharing and information we do not request

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google for advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement, and service operation, and by Apple when you use Game Center. These providers process information under their own policies and your choices.

Arrovia does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information, or payment-card details. The App does not include chat or public user-generated content.

06

Retention, children, and international processing

Local gameplay information remains on your device until it is reset or the App is deleted. Information processed by Google or Apple is retained according to their respective policies.

Arrovia is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent behavior may vary according to region, age-related settings, and applicable law.

Google, Apple, and their service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their stated safeguards and applicable law.

07

  • 05Contact us with a privacy question or request.
