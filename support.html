Common questions

Quick answers.

Why do I see ads?

Ads support Arrovia and help keep the game available. Banner ads may appear during play, and an interstitial may appear after every two completed levels. Rewarded ads are optional and provide a hint when completed.

Can I change my nickname?

Arrovia uses your Apple Game Center identity for supported rankings. You can manage that name through your Game Center settings.

Why is my leaderboard position missing?

Confirm that Game Center is enabled, you are signed in, and your device is online. Scores can sometimes take a short time to appear.

How do I restore or reset progress?

Use the reset option inside Arrovia if you want to start again. Local progress may also be affected when the app is removed or restored through standard iOS backup behavior.

Still need help?
